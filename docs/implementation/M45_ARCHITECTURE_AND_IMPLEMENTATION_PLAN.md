## 1. Executive summary

### 1.1 Purpose

**`[NORMATIVE IF RATIFIED]`** M45 shall provide a procedurally neutral,
Portfolio Intelligence-owned process that:

1. verifies its authority and the frozen M1–M44 baseline;
2. receives only already-authorized, independently confirmed, frozen external
   owner-domain evidence;
3. determines whether the evidence can satisfy frozen `G-3` criteria without
   inference or amendment;
4. issues a distinct prospective checkpoint that may validly be `STOP`;
5. only after a separate substantive-work authorization, an independently
   confirmed authorizing checkpoint, and bounded entry verification, authors
   and freezes the two universal Portfolio Analytics documentary contracts;
   and
6. truthfully closes out either the intended path or a fail-closed path.

M45 does not define success as `G-3 CLOSED`. Its duty is to determine whether
closure is warranted. A correctly evidenced `STOP`, retained
`G-3 OPEN — PARTIAL`, unavailable owner evidence, or non-approved review is a
valid and successful procedural outcome when recorded exactly.

### 1.2 Long-term roadmap position

**`[INFORMATIVE]`** M42 fixed Portfolio Intelligence subject and declaration
contracts. M43 allocated the universal analytics semantics and result
contracts. M44 tested their constitutional entry conditions, recorded the
remaining canonical-form gaps, and ended at an independently confirmed
historic `STOP`. M45 is a possible successor governance vehicle, not a resumed
M44 work package.

### 1.3 Why M45 follows M44

M44 established three facts that a successor must preserve:

- `G-3` is `OPEN — PARTIAL`;
- the historic M44 checkpoint is validly `STOP`; and
- M44 authority is exhausted.

M45 therefore follows M44 only temporally and evidentially. It may act only if
separate milestone allocation, planning ratification, and first-work-package
authorization exist. It never changes the truth of M44.

## 2. Architectural objectives

### 2.1 Primary goals

**`[NORMATIVE IF RATIFIED]`**

1. Preserve deterministic, byte-exact, owner-supplied evidence.
2. Keep external owner-domain action outside M45 control.
3. Make every authority transition explicit and fail closed.
4. Apply independent review, correction, confirmation, identity validation,
   and freeze before any M45-owned substantive artifact becomes effective.
5. Preserve Components A–K as one atomic normative semantic scope.
6. Keep the Portfolio Measure Result contract strictly downstream of frozen
   A–K semantics.
7. Preserve `G-4` only as named annualization unavailability.
8. Provide truthful terminal recording for both intended and blocked paths.

### 2.2 Conditions examined, not predetermined outcomes

| Frozen or inherited condition | Neutral M45 treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Eight `G-3` elements lack complete owner-supplied canonical forms | Receive qualifying frozen artifacts if they independently come into existence; otherwise stop |
| Portfolio Composition, `PMS1`, and `PAIM1` are not completely formable | Test formability only from accepted evidence; never manufacture closure |
| `G-3 OPEN — PARTIAL` | Retain unless every frozen objective criterion is proved |
| Historic M44 checkpoint `STOP` | Preserve as valid historic truth and predecessor boundary |
| `G-4 OPEN` | Bind only as named annualization unavailability |
| `G-5 OPEN` | Close only if both content obligations are independently confirmed and frozen |
| G-2 final Decision Log recording is outstanding | Observe the fact; do not self-authorize a vehicle |

### 2.3 Success criteria

**`[NORMATIVE IF RATIFIED]`** Architectural success means:

- exact authority and identity evidence exists for every act performed;
- external owner evidence is neither requested nor governed by M45;
- every M45-owned artifact completes its review lifecycle before freeze;
- every gate and checkpoint disposition follows the evidence, including
  `STOP`;
- no closure claim rests on a partial, routed, inferred, normalized, or
  implementation-shaped form;
- A–K remain atomic and the Result contract remains downstream; and
- closeout reports unresolved blockers without converting them into closure.

### 2.4 Explicit non-goals

M45 does not:

- amend, reopen, correct, re-freeze, or reinterpret M1–M44;
- treat the historic `STOP` as an error;
- request, commission, schedule, govern, review, confirm, or freeze work in
  Ledger & Accounting, Asset Foundation, Connectivity & Ingestion, or any
  external owner process;
- author the missing Investment Universe or Benchmark forms inside the M45
  work-package chain;
- settle `OQ-5` or write the Decision Log without separately evidenced
  competent authority;
- create runtime code, schemas, migrations, APIs, UI, providers, executable
  fixtures, deployment changes, or production methods;
- supply an annualization factor, placeholder, alias, or synthetic dependency;
  or
- authorize a later milestone.

## 8. Review, correction, confirmation, and freeze model

### 8.1 Lifecycle for every M45-owned substantive artifact

```text
DRAFT
  -> REVIEW CANDIDATE
  -> APPROVED or CORRECTIONS REQUIRED
  -> (additive RCn correction + focused re-review)*
  -> CONFIRMED
  -> content-identity validation
  -> FROZEN
```

No candidate freezes before independent review and confirmation. The primary
author cannot independently review or confirm their own artifact.

### 8.2 Correction rule

Before freeze, corrections create a new candidate revision identity. After
freeze, a defect can be discharged only by an additive successor `RCn`
artifact with its own review, confirmation, identity validation, and freeze
record; the frozen revision is never edited, reopened, or re-frozen.
A downstream package consumes the latest applicable frozen revision and
re-verifies every cited identity.

For an external owner artifact, M45 cannot initiate correction. It records the
finding and stops the dependent branch until a new independently authorized
and frozen owner revision arrives.
