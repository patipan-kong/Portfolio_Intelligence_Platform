## 1. Correction authority and boundary

This response records the supplementary wording correction required by
`M46-RP-IR-F1`. The correction is confined to Resolution Plan statements that
continued to make an additive M46-WP1 successor lifecycle, reconsideration
lifecycle, or successor corpus a mandatory constitutional mechanism after Act
D became mechanism-neutral.

The correction does not modify Act D, redesign the Resolution Plan, change
`ED-01` through `ED-04`, alter a dependency or gate, change authority, prescribe
an implementation mechanism, alter the frozen planning corpus, allocate or
authorize WP2, or perform implementation.

The only existing artifact modified is the Resolution Plan. This supplementary
response is the only artifact created.

## 2. Corrected consistency points

All remaining mandatory WP1 successor/reconsideration language was replaced
with Act D's constitutional result:

- constitutionally admissible intended-path predecessor supply; or
- an already-governed equivalent boundary where constitutionally admissible.

The correction was propagated through the Resolution Plan's executive summary,
dependency inventory and preserved-boundary description, owner-domain mapping,
required repository evidence, dependency graph, recommended execution order,
and earliest allocation-reconsideration condition.

The edits preserve the existing frozen blocked WP1 corpus, the exact evidence
qualities required by Act D, competent satisfaction of `M46-G1`, and the stop at
the WP2 allocation-reconsideration boundary. References to successor mechanisms
that belong to `ED-01`, `ED-02`, or `ED-03` remain because F1 concerns only the
formerly mandated M46-WP1 mechanism and does not authorize dependency changes.

## 3. Constitutional effect

The Resolution Plan now states the same mechanism-neutral requirement wherever
it describes `ED-04`: an admissible intended-path predecessor must be supplied,
or an already-governed equivalent boundary may be cited where constitutionally
admissible. No successor lifecycle, reconsideration lifecycle, successor corpus,
document name, repository layout, or implementation mechanism is required.

The correction changes no required constitutional outcome. `M46-G1` remains
`OPEN` until competent evidence satisfies it, and WP2 remains `ALLOCATION
WITHHELD`.
