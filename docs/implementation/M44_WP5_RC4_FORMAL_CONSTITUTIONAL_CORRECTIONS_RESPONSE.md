## 6. Provenance and its limits

Four provenance matters are disclosed rather than concealed.

**First, this response inherits the RC4 record's disclosed reconstruction
limit, and that limit reaches the corrections this record undertakes.** The
filed RC4 review record discloses at its §3.1 that the original RC4 review
narrative does not survive in any retrievable session output; that what
survives and is carried through is the review's **result** — the determination
`NOT APPROVED`, the RC3 disposition totals, and the identifier, classification,
and stated condition of each active finding, together with the counts; and
that the per-finding constitutional rationale, the per-finding *exact
correction required*, the per-axis assessment, and the confirmation-readiness
wording are that record's **reconstruction**, marked as such where they
appear.

The consequence for this response is direct and is stated plainly. The
identifiers, classifications, conditions, counts, and determination this
record responds to are the review's own. **But the exact correction wording
this record undertakes to implement for `RC4-MAJOR-1` and `RC3-MINOR-4` is
reconstructed wording, not the RC4 reviewer's own.** The corrections
undertaken at §4.2 and §4.3 are framed against those reconstructed
requirements. A later independent reviewer is not bound by them and may
require something different or something more. This is a further and
independent reason why neither finding could be recorded `RESOLVED` on the
author's assessment, and why the undertaken corrections are stated as the
author's response to the filed record rather than as compliance with a
reviewer's instruction.

**Second, the RC4 record's filing was not author-independent, and neither is
this response.** The RC4 record discloses at its §3.1 and §11 point 7 that the
filing act was performed by the party that authored the reviewed candidate,
even though the review being filed was conducted under an author-independent,
read-only posture. This response is authored by the same party. Neither the
filing nor this response is an independent act, and neither carries review
authority.

**Third, `RC4-CRITICAL-1`'s repository act inherits the limits the filed
responses disclose.** The RC2 corrections response filed at `e02a50b`
discloses that it inherits the RC2 review record's reconstruction limit, the
original RC2 narrative not having been preserved. The RC3 corrections response

**Fourth, the evidence in this record is mechanical, not constitutional.** The
