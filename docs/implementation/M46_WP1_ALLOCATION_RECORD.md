## 3. Scope of M46-WP1

The package allocated is exactly:

**`M46-WP1 — Baseline, constitutional reconciliation, vocabulary, and
acceptance-vector contract`.**

Its bounded purpose is to establish the exact M46 execution baseline, verify
the recorded Asset Foundation ownership alignment, close or truthfully block
on the remaining ratification/textual-conformance residual, disposition
candidate vocabulary through its competent owners, and lock generic
acceptance vectors.

The prospective WP1 scope is limited to:

- repository and current-state inventory;
- frozen-predecessor identity verification, including AF-WP1 through AF-WP4;
- recorded-alignment and residual-evidence intake;
- a vocabulary ownership and disposition register;
- generic positive, boundary, negative, correction, and migration vectors;
- BANPU fixture parameters as evidence slots only; and
- the documentary registers, matrices, and explicit fail-closed disposition
  named for WP1 in the frozen roadmap.

The frozen roadmap's WP1 exclusions remain controlling: no corporate-action
adjudication contract, real-action adjudication, Asset or Ledger fact,
runtime-inventory mutation, M46 glossary admission, BANPU terms, ratio, alias,
or correction is in scope.
