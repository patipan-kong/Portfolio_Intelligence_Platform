M-1 — §1.1 asserts as a consequence the proposition it disclaims deciding.

§1.1 states: "This planning document does not decide whether OQ-3(c) can form an OPEN record while the exact-owner requirements remain unsatisfied." Four lines later it concludes: "the ownership determination fails under §10, the §11 completion criteria are not met, and no G-4 terminal state is established."

The first two conjuncts follow directly from frozen text (§10's failure row; §11's conjunctive completion criteria). The third does not — "no G-4 terminal state is established" holds only if OQ-3(c) cannot form an ownerless OPEN record, which is the very question disclaimed. As written, §1.1 adopts one branch's operational conclusion while stating it takes no side.

§3 already contains the precise formulation and should govern the document: "The frozen tension quoted in §1.1 prevents WP5 from forming a terminal G-4 record without resolving constitutional meaning outside its authority." That is an authority-based inability, not a merits ruling, and it is correct.

Weight. Presentational, not substantive. Under either reading of the tension, WP5 cannot produce a conforming record without either inventing an owner value (prohibited — RC2 J-2) or resolving the tension (no authority — RC2 C-1). Downstream effect is identical in both readings: with frozen G-3 OPEN — PARTIAL, §12.1.1 row 2 yields Stop, or formally re-scope regardless. No authority, state, or authorization turns on the wording.

M-2 — §12.1.1 is invoked on a branch where its own entry condition is unmet, and "the defect" shifts referent.

Two coupled precision defects in §§1.1, 3, 5 (WP5.2), 5.1, and 8:

Entry condition. Frozen §12.1.1 runs "After M44-WP4 and M44-WP5 are confirmed and before M44-WP6 begins." On the ownership-proof-failure branch RC3 correctly holds that WP5 never confirms — so the checkpoint is never reached. §5.1 therefore asserts both that the checkpoint is triggered by "WP5 confirmation and freeze" and that ownership-proof failure "implicates that outcome." The intended reading is presumably that row 3's rule supplies the governing principle even where the checkpoint is not reached; the document does not say so.

Referent. Frozen §12.1.1 row 3 locates the defect precisely: "a review defect in the producing work package and is corrected before the checkpoint is re-evaluated." RC3 then writes "the defect can be corrected only by a new architecture revision that names the defect" — silently shifting the referent from a WP5 defect to a frozen-architecture defect. These are two different defects under two different remedies. Note that frozen row 2, not row 3, is the row carrying the architecture-revision remedy.

Weight. The architecture-revision remedy is correctly and independently grounded in Freeze Record §9 and §1.6 rules 1–2, which RC3 cites. The citations are sound; only the prose conflates the two defect classes. The error is conservative in direction — it adds a governance precondition rather than removing one.

M-3 — INCAPABLE OF PASSING THE FUTURE GATE is a novel token with no frozen source, diverging from established M44 practice.

§§0 (RC1 J-8), 4.1, and 7 require illustrative examples to carry the labels ARTIFICIAL, NON-EFFECTIVE, and INCAPABLE OF PASSING THE FUTURE GATE. The first two are established across the frozen corpus. The third appears nowhere outside this planning document — a repository-wide search returns only its three occurrences here.

The established triple in the sibling M44 artifact and its frozen predecessors is ARTIFICIAL / NON-EFFECTIVE / NON-CONFORMANCE-ESTABLISHING, used throughout M44_WP4_POSITIVE_DOCUMENTARY_VECTORS.md and frozen M43-WP5 §§431, 656, 770, 819, 1073 and M43-WP7 §752.

This sits uneasily beside the header's Vocabulary-admission authority: NONE and §8's own control requiring any new governed noun to pass the five-part vocabulary gate.

Weight. A documentary vector label is not a governed business noun, and label-coining without a vocabulary gate is established non-governed practice within M44 (WP4 coined NON-CONFORMANCE-ESTABLISHING the same way). The requirement is also stricter than anything frozen §11 M44-WP5 "Required tests" or WP1 §4.4 imposes. The defect is corpus inconsistency, not authority overreach.
