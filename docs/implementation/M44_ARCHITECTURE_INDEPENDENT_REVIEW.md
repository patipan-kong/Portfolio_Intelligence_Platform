Overall constitutional assessment
The proposal is not constitutionally consistent. Its core gate-closure mechanism conflicts with frozen M42/M43 authority boundaries and contains sequencing that can declare gates closed without their required preconditions.
1. CRITICAL — WP4’s byte-encoding contract contradicts frozen M42-WP7
Repository evidence: [M44 plan §8.3 and §11 WP4 (line 750)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:750) authorizes an “exact canonical byte representation.” Frozen [M42-WP7 §2 (line 50)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M42_WP7_PORTFOLIO_COMPOSITION_CONTRACT_SPECIFICATION.md:50) excludes byte encoding; [§5 (line 179)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M42_WP7_PORTFOLIO_COMPOSITION_CONTRACT_SPECIFICATION.md:179) expressly states it defines no byte or character encoding; and [negative vectors (line 233)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M42_WP7_PORTFOLIO_COMPOSITION_CONTRACT_SPECIFICATION.md:233) classify prescribing a byte encoding as a constitutional defect. M42-WP7 also grants downstream consumers no authority to operationalize or alter the composition boundary ([§6 (line 187)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M42_WP7_PORTFOLIO_COMPOSITION_CONTRACT_SPECIFICATION.md:187)).
Why it is a problem: This is not “declared silence” under G3. The frozen contract expressly withholds this authority and marks byte encoding as out of scope/non-conforming. M44 cannot treat that prohibition as a delegated implementation gap while claiming all frozen artifacts remain authoritative.
Recommended correction: Remove WP4’s claimed encoding authority from M44. Obtain a separately authorized, constitutionally valid extension or superseding contract that explicitly delegates canonical-byte representation while preserving M42-WP7’s semantic boundary. Do not permit WP6/WP7 to proceed on Composition bytes until that authority exists.
2. CRITICAL — WP5 can create or admit an annualization dependency without the separately authorized owner-side instrument required by M43-WP4
Repository evidence: M44-WP5 permits a dependency contract and anticipates “potentially one new governed contract-kind registration in the owning domain’s corpus” ([§11 (line 1086)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:1086)). Frozen [M43-WP4 (line 60)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M43_WP4_CONSTITUTIONAL_SCOPE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:60) says WP4 cannot invent the required existing governed contract kind and requires a separately authorized governance instrument to supply the owner, kind, identifier, version, and bytes. Its dependency rules prohibit artificial or WP4-authored dependency kinds ([§5.2 (line 329)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M43_WP4_CONSTITUTIONAL_SCOPE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:329)).
Why it is a problem: M44 has no Market Intelligence or cross-domain contract-authoring authority. An ownership proof cannot itself create the missing owner-side contract kind. Recording a permanent blockage also does not close the annualization gate for affected annualized claims.
Recommended correction: Split this from M44’s gate-closure claim. Require the owner domain’s separately authorized governance instrument before any annualization dependency contract is admitted. If it does not exist, retain G-4 as open and prohibit WP6/WP7 from claiming annualization closure; only unaffected semantics may proceed.
3. MAJOR — WP4’s partial-routing outcome is incompatible with the claimed G-3 closure and downstream sequence
Repository evidence: M44 permits each coordinate to be “closed or explicitly routed” ([WP4 completion criteria (line 1078)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:1078)), and OQ-1 accepts partial closure or re-scoping ([§17 (line 1488)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:1488)). Yet it schedules WP6/WP7 after WP4 as though G-3 is closed ([§12.3 (line 1249)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:1249)). Frozen M43-WP3 states that if an owner cannot provide the required canonical reference/representation, no conforming subject can be formed ([Subject contract (line 275)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M43_WP3_PORTFOLIO_MEASURE_SUBJECT_CONTRACT_SPECIFICATION.md:275)); the Manifest similarly prohibits a concrete manifest ([Manifest contract (line 337)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M43_WP3_PORTFOLIO_ANALYTICS_INPUT_MANIFEST_CONTRACT_SPECIFICATION.md:337)).
Why it is a problem: Routing an unresolved coordinate does not make a Composition, Subject, Manifest, Result identity, or canonical-byte vector formable. The plan conflates an honestly recorded blockage with closure.
Recommended correction: Make successful closure of every required coordinate an explicit hard prerequisite for WP6/WP7. If any coordinate is unresolved, M44 must stop after documenting the blockage or be formally re-scoped before normative result work begins.
4. MAJOR — G-2 is declared closed before the frozen correction path’s required ratification vehicle exists
Repository evidence: Frozen M43-WP1 requires the final resolution to be recorded in the consolidated Decision Log entry authorized at M43 epic closeout ([§7.4 (line 492)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M43_WP1_PORTFOLIO_ANALYTICS_VOCABULARY_AND_OWNERSHIP_REGISTER.md:492)). M44-WP3 declares itself the G-2 closure and downstream entry gate ([§11 (line 1014)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:1014)), but defers its Decision Log ratification until M44 epic closeout ([§12.6 (line 1278)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:1278)). M44 itself identifies this as unresolved in OQ-5 ([§17 (line 1569)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:1569)).
Why it is a problem: The proposal assumes a future M44 Decision Log entry can discharge a step explicitly authorized for the already-closed M43 epic, but has no established authority for that substitution. It would allow D-1 to begin before the claimed ratification occurs.
Recommended correction: Resolve the governing mechanism before approval—through an authorized level-3 decision or an explicit governance determination that supersedes the unavailable M43 vehicle. Until then, WP3 may document the defect but must not mark G-2 closed or release D-1.
5. MAJOR — Provider-boundary failure behavior contradicts mandatory M39/M41 evidence consumption
Repository evidence: M44 requires Market Observations and Market Measure Results as the only admissible market evidence ([§6 (line 599)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:599)), and identifies market evidence, benchmark series, FX, calendars, and risk-free observations as consumed dependencies ([§5.2 (line 425)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:425)). But its failure matrix says a provider-sourced value reaching an M44 boundary is invalid and rejected ([§10 (line 939)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:939)).
Why it is a problem: Canonical M39 evidence may have external/provider provenance while remaining valid platform evidence. The current wording can reject the very canonical evidence required for benchmark, FX, calendar, and risk-free dependencies.
Recommended correction: Distinguish prohibited raw provider payload/symbol/default from permitted canonical, manifest-bound M39/M41 evidence carrying provenance. Preserve rejection of direct provider semantics without rejecting normalized Market Intelligence evidence.
6. MINOR — Future-milestone labels conflict with the plan’s declared lack of future-work-package allocation authority
Repository evidence: M44 assigns indicative M45–M47 responsibilities ([§4.5 (line 400)](/D:/Works/TA/work/Portfolio_Intelligence_Platform/docs/implementation/M44_ARCHITECTURE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md:400)), including M47 discharging M43-WP9, while immediately declaring it has no future-work-package design authority.
Why it is a problem: Even labeled “indicative,” this creates an architectural allocation signal for a live, unowned M43-WP9 obligation. It risks future milestone-number and ownership conflicts.
Recommended correction: Record only the unresolved successor obligation and its prerequisites. Leave milestone number and allocation to the separately authorized successor architecture.
