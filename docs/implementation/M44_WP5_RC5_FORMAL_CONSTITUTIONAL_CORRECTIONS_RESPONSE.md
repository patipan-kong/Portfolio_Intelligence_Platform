## 5. Response to `RC5-CRITICAL-1`

### 5.1 Original finding posture

The filed RC5 review classified this finding `CRITICAL` and identified the
affected scope as §§1, 6.1, 13, 14 and the confirmation-readiness posture.
It found that the candidate contained only the determination method, supplied
no ownership conclusion, corpus assessment, §8.7 outcome, or completed WP5.5
record, and therefore could not lawfully enter WP5.6 or confirmation.

The review's required correction was not to submit or confirm the method-only
RC5 blob, but to apply the authorized workflow in the same sole deliverable
through WP5.5 and submit the resulting complete applied determination record
to a new whole-record author-independent review. If the determination stopped
under §10.1 or §10.2, that stop was to be preserved and confirmation was not
to be entered.

The filed review states the required correction exactly as follows:

> Do not submit or confirm the method-only RC5 blob. Apply the authorized
> workflow in this same sole deliverable through WP5.5. If that produces a
> lawful §8.7 terminal-state proposal, submit the resulting changed blob—the
> complete applied determination record—to a new whole-record
> author-independent review, followed by confirmation and freeze under §13.
>
> If the determination stops under §10.1 or §10.2, preserve that stop and do
> not enter confirmation. A separate method-confirmation lifecycle may not be
> created by editing this specification; it would require independently
> confirmed frozen authority.

### 5.2 Formal response

The finding was accepted.

The response accepted that no separate method-confirmation lifecycle was
authorized and that the sole integrated WP5 deliverable had to contain the
applied determination through WP5.5 before any lawful WP5.6 entry. The
corrective act was planned for, and later performed within, the same
specification candidate lineage.

The formal disposition is:

**`ADDRESSED — REQUIRES RE-VALIDATION`**

“Addressed” records acceptance and the corrective act undertaken. It does not
mean `RESOLVED`, `APPROVED`, `CONFIRMED`, or constitutionally discharged.
Only a later author-independent whole-record review of the changed candidate
could determine whether the method-only defect had been cured without
regression and whether the candidate was eligible for any subsequent
constitutional stage.
