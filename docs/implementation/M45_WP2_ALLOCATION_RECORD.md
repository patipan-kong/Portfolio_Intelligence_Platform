## Blocking conditions

The following dependencies remain unresolved and block allocation:

1. The external owner-domain canonical forms required for all eight G-3
   elements have not been supplied as independently authorized, reviewed,
   confirmed, content-identifiable, frozen artifacts.
2. The single Base Currency element lacks the required joint, compatible
   Ledger & Accounting and Asset Foundation evidence.
3. The required external Provenance form has not been supplied with its exact
   content representation, boundaries, sequence, and completeness basis.
4. The required lawful Portfolio Intelligence nested-form artifact has not
   been supplied. Existing frozen predecessor boundaries do not authorize
   M45 to infer, repair, normalize, or substitute these forms.

The M44 G-3 Roadmap preserves `G-3` as `OPEN — PARTIAL` and requires these
forms to be supplied by their owning domains through the source-owner
lifecycle. The frozen WP2 roadmap therefore requires both frozen WP1 and the
independent existence of external artifacts before WP2 may execute. The
roadmap's instruction is to await qualifying external artifacts; it does not
permit WP2 to be opened to create, solicit, or replace them.

Accordingly, no WP1 dependency remains unresolved, but the external
prerequisites for WP2 remain unresolved. WP2 is not eligible for allocation.

This record does not authorize WP2 and does not constitute implementation
authority.
