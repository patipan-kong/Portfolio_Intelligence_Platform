## 4. Meaning of the prior `BLOCKED` / `OUTCOME C` determinations

Each of the three predecessor determinations (16:46, 16:57, 17:03) addressed a common, narrower question than the one this act resolves: whether **some role, body, or process already existing inside this repository's governance corpus** — WP5's own authority, an ARB-type jurisdiction, or a self-constituted design-amendment act — was competent to originate new D2/D4/D5/D6 content. Each answered no, on evidence specific to that scope: WP5's authority is bounded and the content is "economically substantive and canonically silent" (16:46); no "Design Owner" or "Architecture Owner" role is named anywhere in the corpus (16:57); no repository-internal process, including a self-constituted one, has ever amended a frozen top-rank design in place, and the one on-point cross-corpus precedent (M44 Finding 2) affirmatively forecloses an adjacent body supplying such content for the owning domain (17:03). None of the three determinations examined, or purported to examine, whether a **real-world external human party outside the repository's governance corpus** — the actual party these determinations themselves describe as the missing "Design Owner" — could subsequently supply that authority directly. That question was outside each determination's own stated scope.

## 5. Whether the prior records foreclosed later external human authorization

**No.** Independently re-read this act: none of the three determinations contains any statement that no external human owner may ever authorize BANPU design content, now or in the future. To the contrary, the 16:57 determination's own named next-act options include a "design-competent clarification act," without specifying its source must be repository-internal; and the 17:03 determination's §15 explicitly identifies "direct action by whatever real-world party actually held the original... authority" as an admissible path it could not itself take, precisely because that party is external to what the determination could constitute for itself. The determinations are best read as jurisdictional self-limitations — this repository's documented governance vocabulary cannot manufacture design-rank authority from nothing — not as a substantive ruling that no such authority could ever exist or act. `No repository-internal authority existed/found` is confirmed distinct from, and does not imply, `no external human owner may ever subsequently authorize anything`.

## 6. Internal vs. external vs. agent authority distinction

- **A. Repository-internal standing authority** — a role, body, or process whose competence is established by pre-existing repository governance text (design, Roadmap, WPP, ARB precedent). The three predecessor determinations correctly found this absent for D2/D4/D5/D6, and that finding is **unchanged and remains historically valid** after this act.
- **B. External human owner authority** — authority exercised directly by the real-world party who held the original design authority, acting outside the repository's pre-existing governance corpus. This is the authority category asserted by the statement recorded in §2, and it is the authority this record treats as now supplied.
- **C. Agent/session execution authority** — the executing agent's capacity to read, analyze, and materialize repository artifacts. This category confers no design-authoring authority of its own, at any point in this corpus's history, including in the creation of `BANPU_MECHANICAL_CONTINUITY_DESIGN_CLARIFICATION.md` itself: that artifact's own text already disclaimed agent-originated authority (per its self-declared provenance line), and this record does not revise that disclaimer or newly attribute D2–D6 authorship to the agent.
