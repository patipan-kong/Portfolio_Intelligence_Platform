## 3. Executive conclusion

The candidate is, in most respects, a careful and constitutionally literate
piece of work. Its decision-language convention, its refusal of runtime and
implementation authority, its atomicity rule for Components A–K, its strict
WP9/WP10 separation, its preservation of `G-4` as named annualization
unavailability, its explicit statement that a `STOP` disposition is a valid
terminal outcome, and its confinement of navigation synchronization to WP11
are all correct and are affirmed below.

It is nevertheless **`NOT APPROVED`** for ratification, on two `BLOCKING`
grounds and four `MAJOR` grounds.

The two blocking defects share one root: the candidate treats the eight routed
`G-3` elements as work that M45 can *organize*, when frozen M44 established
that they are work M45's own domain cannot *reach*.

1. **`F-1`** — M45-WP2, M45-WP3, and M45-WP5 allocate owned work to Ledger &
   Accounting, Asset Foundation, and Connectivity & Ingestion, complete with
   M45-assigned identifiers, M45-defined deliverables, and M45-defined exit
   criteria. Frozen M44 records that Portfolio Intelligence "reaches no domain
   but Portfolio Intelligence" (INV-C4), that soliciting canonical references
   from owning domains is "constitutionally unavailable," that the routing
   table "is a record, not a request," and that the owning domains'
   "cooperation M44 cannot compel." The candidate never establishes that M45
   holds the cross-domain authority M44 lacked; its own header declares no
   cross-domain authority at all.

2. **`F-2`** — M45-WP4 proposes that Portfolio Intelligence supply the
   Investment Universe nested form and the three Benchmark facets. Frozen
   M44-WP1 §6.6 recorded that supplying exactly those forms "may constitute a
   silent amendment of frozen M42," referred the question, and frozen
   `WP4-NR-032` then resolved it in the negative. The candidate asserts the
   opposite conclusion — that the forms can be supplied additively — without
   citing, engaging, or distinguishing that frozen resolution.

The four `MAJOR` findings concern the G-2 recording vehicle (`F-3`), the
absence of any stated authority for allocating the M45 identifier and for
ratifying the architecture (`F-4`), the absence of any correction path when a
downstream review finds a defect in an already-frozen upstream package
(`F-5`), and language in §1.1/§2.2 that presupposes `G-3 CLOSED` and frames
the frozen `STOP` as a problem M45 solves (`F-6`).

None of these findings is an objection to M45 as a concept. Each is a defect
in the candidate's account of its own authority. All ten non-advisory findings
are correctable within documentary planning scope.

### 8.1 On WP1's competence for the G-2 final recording

The review objective asked this question specifically. The answer is that WP1
as drafted is **not** a demonstrated competent vehicle, for three independent
reasons:

1. **The obligation is intrinsically a Decision Log recording.** Frozen
   M43-WP1 §7.4 step 4 reads: "the final resolution is recorded in the
   consolidated Decision Log entry authorized at M43 epic closeout." The
   originally named vehicle lapsed; frozen M44 RC2 §17 `OQ-5` recommended the
   M44 consolidated Decision Log entry as substitute "but M44 does not
   self-authorize it"; that M44 entry was subsequently written and frozen, and
   the M44 Epic Closeout Freeze Record §5 states plainly that it "is not an
   authorized substitute recording vehicle and does not close G-2." A vehicle
   for step 4 is therefore still, in form, a Decision Log entry.

2. **The candidate forbids WP1 from writing one.** Architecture §11 criterion
   19 permits Decision Log synchronization "only after final confirmation and
   under explicit synchronization authority"; §9.4 lists "synchronization only
   in WP11" as a control; §12.1 confines the planning stage to two files.
   Roadmap §11 gives WP11 sole Decision Log scope. WP1 deliverable 4's
   "additive recording artifact" is either a Decision Log entry — in which case
   it violates all four of those statements — or it is not, in which case it
   does not discharge step 4 and should not be described as performing the
   recording.

3. **Nothing identifies who may settle `OQ-5`.** The M44 Epic Closeout
   Independent Constitutional Confirmation §8 conditions any vehicle on "an
   authority that can settle OQ-5," and names none. The candidate inherits that
   silence and then places the assessment inside the very work package that
   would act on it. WP1 would decide its own competence and then exercise it —
   the precise self-authorization M44 twice declined.

The correction is not to abandon the G-2 recording. It is to split the two
acts: WP1 may determine and record *whether* a competent vehicle exists and
what it would be; performing the recording must be a separately authorized act
placed where the candidate's own rules already put governance-record writes.

**Explanation.** The candidate assigns M45 identifiers to work owned by three

place. The candidate's header declares seven authority classes as `NONE` and
