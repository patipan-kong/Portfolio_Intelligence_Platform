## 1. Roadmap identity and status

This roadmap describes the evidence and governance sequence required before a
future competent governance authority could establish a valid `G-3` closure
and admit M44-WP6 to its frozen entry boundary. It is not a `G-3` closure,
checkpoint authorization, normative WP6 authoring, implementation, runtime
work, or successor-identifier allocation.

The governing correction is:

> Frozen M44 cannot itself reopen or close G-3; any later closure requires
> separately authorized governance.

Future identity is intentionally unspecified. This roadmap does not require a
future process to leave the M44 constitutional family, does not authorize any
particular successor process, and does not allocate a milestone or work-package
identifier.

## 3. Authority exhaustion and future governance

### 3.1 Frozen M44 authority exhaustion

Frozen M44 records the existing non-closure state, the owner-domain routing of
the missing forms, and the historic `STOP`. It does not itself supply, solicit,
invent, repair, reinterpret, or select any missing owner-domain canonical
form. Routing is evidence of an unresolved ownership boundary, not supply and
not a closure.

Accordingly, the historic `STOP` is a valid frozen conclusion of its own
checkpoint. It cannot be silently bypassed by treating the roadmap, a routing
table, a specimen, or an implementation as closure evidence.

### 3.2 Future separately authorized governance

Separately authorized future governance may establish a competent process to
receive owner-domain evidence, determine whether the `G-3` closure criteria
are met, and issue a distinct future checkpoint disposition. Its identity,
scope, and authorization are not created by this roadmap.

That process must preserve all frozen authority. It may neither rewrite the
historic `STOP` nor treat a new disposition as a retroactive amendment of the
frozen M44 checkpoint.

## 5. Required source-owner lifecycle

For each required canonical form, the authorized successor process must preserve
the following source-owner lifecycle before treating the form as closure
evidence:

1. **Authoring** — The owning domain authorizes and publishes the exact
   canonical form, including its applicable identifier, immutable version, and
   canonical bytes or deterministically citable byte definition.
2. **Review** — An independent reviewer verifies owner authority, source
   exactness, field-and-facet coverage, and non-conflict with frozen M42, M43,
   and M44 authority.
3. **Confirmation** — An independent confirmer verifies the review result,
   canonical identity, cited evidence, and absence of unclosed constitutional
   or serialization findings.
4. **Freeze** — The accepted owner-domain artifact and its evidence identity
   are frozen before they are used as a basis for `G-3` closure.

The lifecycle must retain a traceable evidence chain from each frozen source
form to its use in the complete Composition representation. A record of a
routed gap, without that lifecycle, is not closure evidence.

## 6. Successor G-3 closure package requirements

Without assigning it a filename, milestone number, or work-package number, an
authorized successor process must assemble a closure package that contains:

1. a complete eight-element evidence matrix, with exact owner, source path or
   immutable locator, identifier, version, byte or byte-definition identity,
   and field/facet coverage for each form;
2. proof that every required nested form is owner-supplied and is neither
   invented, defaulted, inferred, normalized, nor derived from presentation or
   implementation material;
3. a complete Portfolio Composition construction using those source-owned
   forms as opaque canonical inputs;
4. proof that complete Composition bytes and concrete `PMS1` and `PAIM1`
   instances become formable from the supplied evidence;
5. a closure finding that applies the frozen `G-3` condition at every required
   field and facet, rather than declaring closure from a routing or partial
   inventory; and
6. source-to-claim traceability sufficient for independent constitutional and
   serialization review.

The closure package is not a new source of canonical semantics. It must cite
and preserve canonical forms supplied under the required owner lifecycle.

## 7. Independent constitutional and serialization review

Before a future `G-3` closure may be confirmed, the closure package requires
independent review on both boundaries:

| Review boundary | Required finding |
| --- | --- |
| Constitutional review | The package uses only competent owner-domain authority; preserves every frozen M42, M43, and M44 decision; makes no implied successor allocation; and does not amend the historic `STOP`. |
| Serialization review | Required source forms are exact, immutable, complete, and deterministic; Composition construction preserves opaque owner bytes, ordering, owner associations, Provenance associations, and affirmative absence. |
| Independent-reader derivation | Two independent readers, using the same frozen evidence, derive byte-identical complete Composition bytes and byte-identical concrete `PMS1` and `PAIM1` instances. |

An unresolved constitutional, ownership, exactness, completeness, or
serialization finding prevents a closure determination. Independent review is
not itself confirmation, freeze, or checkpoint authorization.

## 8. Distinct future checkpoint requirements

After owner evidence has completed its lifecycle and a closure package has
passed independent review and confirmation, future competent governance must
conduct a distinct checkpoint. That checkpoint must:

1. re-verify every `G-3` closure-evidence item and its frozen identity;
2. confirm that the source-owner lifecycle, constitutional review, and
   serialization review are complete and have no unresolved findings;
3. verify that the historic M44 `STOP` remains preserved as a historical,
   frozen disposition rather than being amended or ignored;
4. record whether the new evidence establishes `G-3 CLOSED`; and
5. be independently confirmed before it can serve as an authorizing
   disposition for WP6 entry.

The future checkpoint is distinct from the historic M44 checkpoint. A new
authorizing outcome would operate prospectively; it would not reverse, correct,
or invalidate the frozen historic `STOP`.

## 9. Exact WP6-0 entry-verification boundary

The frozen M44-WP6 plan defines WP6-0 as a verification boundary, not an
authoring stage. WP6-0 may pass only when it verifies all of the following:

1. a separately authorized future governance act explicitly authorizes
   substantive M44-WP6 work;
2. `G-3 CLOSED` evidence is valid, complete, independently confirmed, and
   traceable to frozen owner-domain forms;
3. a distinct independently confirmed authorizing checkpoint disposition
   exists;
4. M44-WP4 and M44-WP5 remain frozen and their cited outputs resolve at their
   exact canonical paths; and
5. the historic `STOP` is not bypassed by implication.

If any condition is absent, WP6-0 stops. It may not create missing references,
close a gate, author a normative row, supply a source-owned form, or cure an
authority defect.

## 12. Objective G-3 closure criteria

`G-3 CLOSED` may be determined only when all of the following objective
criteria are satisfied by separately authorized future governance:

1. every one of the eight recorded open elements has an exact, immutable,
   owner-supplied canonical form with complete field-and-facet determinacy;
2. each form has completed authoring, independent review, independent
   confirmation, and freeze under the source-owner lifecycle;
3. the evidence matrix establishes complete ownership, source identity,
   version, canonical-byte or byte-definition identity, and traceability;
4. a complete Composition representation is formable without defaults,
   inference, substitution, normalization, or synthetic bytes;
5. concrete `PMS1` and `PAIM1` instances are formable from the complete
   Composition bytes;
6. two independent readers derive byte-identical bytes from the same frozen
   evidence; and
7. independent constitutional and serialization review finds no unresolved
   matter that defeats closure.

These criteria establish the evidentiary basis for a future gate finding. They
do not by themselves authorize normative WP6 authoring; the distinct checkpoint
and WP6-0 boundary remain required.

## 13. Constitutional permission boundary for normative WP6 authoring

WP6 normative authoring may begin only after all of the following have occurred
in order:

1. separately authorized future governance has authorized substantive WP6
   work;
2. `G-3` has been validly established as `CLOSED` using the objective criteria
   in this roadmap and frozen owner-domain evidence;
3. a distinct future checkpoint has issued an authorizing disposition and has
   been independently confirmed; and
4. WP6-0 has successfully verified the frozen entry conditions.

At that point, `G-4 OPEN — EFFECTIVE AND FROZEN` remains bindable only as named
annualization unavailability. It does not block WP6 entry once the `G-3` and
checkpoint conditions above are satisfied, and it does not authorize a value,
factor, alias, placeholder, or synthetic dependency. Components A–K remain one
atomic normative scope.

## 15. Final roadmap conclusion

G-3 remains OPEN — PARTIAL. The historic M44 checkpoint STOP remains valid and
frozen. G-4 remains OPEN — EFFECTIVE AND FROZEN. G-4 is bindable only as named
annualization unavailability. Components A–K remain one atomic normative scope.
