# Canonical instrument context v1

`wealth.optimizer-instrument-context.v1` is a read-only model-context projection, not a classifier, policy evaluator or allocation algorithm.

Producer path: `analyze_optimizer` batch-resolves the union of scored symbols and exact held symbols with `resolve_execution_instruments`. The existing facts adapter derives DR form from outgoing `DEPOSITARY_RECEIPT_OF` relationships. `build_instrument_context` consumes those shared facts, reads underlying Asset identity by the supplied canonical ID, and includes the existing execution metadata. It never fetches quotes or derives identities from ticker patterns. Missing or ambiguous classifications and missing underlying identities are explicit; legacy execution assumptions remain labeled with their existing source.

Each held-position entry captures held symbol, local Asset ID, instrument form and resolution status, classification provenance, local quote currency, canonical underlying identity/currency when available, execution profile/risk/cap metadata, and unresolved evidence. Generic Registry EQUITY is not used as independent evidence against DR form. Underlying identity is Registry evidence; the relationship schema does not retain the external listing-document provenance or a complete historical validity timeline.

Consumer path: the prepared context's exact `prompt_block` is shared by L1, L2 and L3 via `build_execution_prompt_block`. L1's minimal retry and the single-shot fallback also receive it. Classification is factual context, not an automatic BUY/SELL instruction. L3 keeps calculated policy breaches, owner-Intent disagreements and advisory hypotheses distinct.

Rules remain separate: existing per-symbol execution caps clamp BUY/ACCUMULATE targets only in primary layered finalization; baseline DR cap 15%, HIGH/CRITICAL cap 10%. Existing HOLD/REDUCE/SELL semantics and fallback enforcement remain unchanged. The 40% DR basket threshold is prompt-only advisory guidance, not deterministic basket enforcement or a calculated breach. General portfolio policy continues through its separate active-policy context. No thresholds, scoring formulas, policy calculations or allocations were changed by this slice.

The context is attached under `execution_context.canonical_instrument_context` before AI calls. It includes a version, the exact shared prompt block and its SHA-256. The existing unconditional `OptimizerHistory.result_json` boundary captures it; RecommendationSnapshot links to that history through `optimizer_history_id`. Historical context is obtained from stored JSON, never today's Registry. Earlier results without this context remain without it: no backfill, migration or historical reconstruction. History 217 and snapshot 173 are untouched. Capture describes supplied context, not a guarantee that a provider accepted it or followed its instructions.

Fixture tests cover four DR relationships, ordinary Thai equity, unresolved classification, absent underlying evidence, local THB versus underlying USD, shared L1/L2/L3 serialization, retry/fallback serialization, unconditional history capture and historical reads without Registry recomputation. Existing Advisory integration/dogfood regressions protect owner-Intent conflict and deferred execution semantics. No live provider calls are needed.
